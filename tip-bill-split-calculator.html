How Does Tip & Bill Splitting Work?

Splitting a restaurant check when eating out with friends, family, or colleagues can quickly become awkward. Here is the clean math used to divide the bill fairly:

1

Calculate Tip Amount

Multiply the food bill by the chosen percentage (e.g., $100 bill × 18% = $18 tip).

2

Add to Total Check

Add food check + tip together to arrive at the total restaurant payment ($100 + $18 = $118).

3

Divide by People

Divide total by the group count so everyone chips in an exact fair share ($118 ÷ 4 = $29.50 each).

Standard Dining Tipping Etiquette

15% - Standard Service

Good for casual dining, cafe meals, lunches, and average table service.

18% - 20% - Great Service

The modern expectation for full-service sit-down restaurants, dinner dates, and group dining.

20%+ - Exceptional

For outstanding hospitality, large group orders, and holiday dining.
