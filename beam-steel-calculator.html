Bar Bending Schedule (BBS) & Civil Rules

In reinforced cement concrete (RCC) construction, accurate rebar estimation prevents site wastage and steel theft while ensuring structural compliance with IS 456 and IS 2502 standards.

⚖️ Density & Unit Weight Formula

Steel density is 7,850 kg/m³. Rebar weight per running meter is derived as: w = (π/4 × D² × 7850) / 1000000 = D² / 162.28 kg/m.

📏 Development Length (Ld)

The standard hook / 90° L-bend anchoring into supporting columns provides safe tension transfer, typically taken between 200mm to 350mm (or 40d to 50d).

🏗️ Commercial Standard Length

Rebar is commercially supplied in 12-meter (40 ft) standard lengths. Tonnage is measured in Metric Tonnes (1 MT = 1,000 KG).
