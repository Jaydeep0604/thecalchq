How Chronological Age Is Calculated (With Leap Years)

Standard chronological age measures the exact elapsed calendar time since your moment of birth. Because months vary between 28, 29, 30, and 31 days, and leap years introduce an extra February 29 every 4 years, a simple 365-day division produces fractional errors. Our algorithm borrows precisely from month end-dates to guarantee 100% legal precision for passport, visa, and civil service applications.

🏛️

Government & Exam Cutoffs

Civil services, SSC, UPSC, and defense exams calculate age as of a specific cutoff date rather than the current date.

🩺

Chronological vs Biological Age

Chronological age is based on your birth certificate, whereas biological age is determined by biomarkers, cellular health, and telomere length.
