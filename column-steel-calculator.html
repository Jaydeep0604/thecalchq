Engineering & Costing Formulas Used

Unit Weight (kg/m): (Dia × Dia) / 162
Weight/Bar (kg): Cutting Length (m) × Unit Weight
Lapp/Bar (kg): ((Lap Factor × Dia) / 1000) × Unit Weight
Total Column Wt (kg): (Wt/Col + Lapp/Col) × No. of Columns
Total Tonnage (Ton): Total Column Weight (kg) / 1000
Steel Cost: Total Weight (Ton) × Steel Rate (/Ton)
GST (18%): Steel Cost × 0.18
Electricity (0% GST Exempt): Total Weight (Ton) × Electricity Rate (/Ton)
