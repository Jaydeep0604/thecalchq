Margin vs Markup: What's the Difference?

While both metrics express profitability, they use different denominators. Margin is the percentage of final sale price that you keep as profit. Markup is the percentage added on top of your wholesale cost. Confusing the two is one of the most common reasons businesses underprice products.

🎯

Gross Margin Formula

(Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100. A $100 item with $40 profit has a 40% gross margin.

🏷️

Markup Formula

(Profit ÷ Cost) × 100. A $40 profit on a $60 cost represents a 66.7% markup on cost.
