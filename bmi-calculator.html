Understanding Body Mass Index (BMI) & Body Fat

BMI is a screening tool developed by Adolphe Quetelet calculated as weight (kg) ÷ [height (m)]². While BMI offers an excellent statistical baseline for general cardiovascular risk, it does not distinguish between muscle mass and adipose fat tissue. That is why our calculator pairs standard BMI with the US Navy Body Fat equation.

Underweight (< 18.5)

May indicate malnutrition, vitamin deficiency, or decreased immune function.

Normal (18.5 – 24.9)

Optimal ratio for longevity and minimal cardiovascular and metabolic risks.

Overweight (25 – 29.9)

Slightly elevated health risks. Common in strength athletes with high muscle density.

Obese (30+)

Increased risk of hypertension, type 2 diabetes, and sleep apnea.
