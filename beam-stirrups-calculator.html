RCC Beam Stirrup Design & Bending Standards

Stirrups provide essential shear resistance against diagonal tension cracking in reinforced concrete beams and prevent lateral buckling of main longitudinal rebar.

📐 Hook Length & Seismic Standards

Under IS 13920 (Ductile Detailing), stirrup hooks must be bent at 135° with an extension of at least 10 × diameter (or 75 mm) to prevent ring unhooking during earthquake loads.

🛡️ Concrete Clear Cover

IS 456 mandates a minimum clear concrete cover of 25 mm (or the bar diameter) for beams to protect internal rebar from corrosion, carbonation, and fire damage.

⚖️ Bend Deductions in Bar Bending

Bending stretches rebar. For accurate field cutting: deduct 1d for 45°, 2d for 90°, and 3d for 135° bends from gross dimensions to prevent oversized stirrups.
