Why Corporate Meeting Costs Matter

Time is the single most expensive operating expense in knowledge work. When six people attend an hour-long meeting, the organization doesn't spend 1 hour: it spends 6 hours of collective output.

1

Calculate Hourly Aggregate

Multiply headcount by average hourly wage (6 people × $50/hr = $300/hour total room cost).

2

Factor in Duration

Multiply the room cost per hour by the duration (45 minutes = 0.75 hours × $300 = $225).

3

Account for Cadence

A weekly recurring 45-minute sync equals 52 meetings = $11,700 every single year!
