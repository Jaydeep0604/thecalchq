Understanding CTC vs In-Hand Take-Home Salary

CTC (Cost to Company) is the total amount an employer spends on an employee annually. However, your actual monthly bank deposit (In-Hand / Take-Home Pay) is lower because it excludes employee and employer PF contributions, professional tax, gratuity, and income tax (TDS).

🏦

Basic Salary & HRA

Basic pay usually accounts for 40%–50% of your CTC. HRA is typically 40% of Basic (or 50% in metro cities like Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru) and is eligible for tax exemption if you pay rent.

🛡️

Provident Fund (PF)

12% of your Basic salary is deducted as your contribution to the Employee Provident Fund (EPF), which earns government-backed compounding interest toward retirement.

⏱️

Hourly & Freelance Rate

Standard full-time employment is 2,080 hours per year (40 hrs/week × 52 weeks). Use the Hourly converter tab when budgeting freelance projects or hourly contracts.
