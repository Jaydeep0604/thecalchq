How Extra Principal Payments Slay Mortgage Debt

In standard amortization schedules, the bulk of your monthly payment in early years goes strictly toward interest. When you submit extra payments earmarked for principal only, 100% of that money cuts the root balance immediately, preventing compound interest from accumulating for the remaining life of the loan.

⚡

Amortization Front-Loading

On a 30-year loan, early payments can be up to 70–80% interest. Accelerating principal early gives maximal dollar savings.

🏦

Specify "Principal Only"

Ensure your mortgage servicer applies extra payments toward principal rather than prepaying next month's interest.

🔄

Bi-Weekly Payment Hack

Paying half your monthly mortgage every 2 weeks creates 26 half-payments = 13 full payments per year, shaving ~4–6 years off.
