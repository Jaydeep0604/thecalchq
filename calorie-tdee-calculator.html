Understanding BMR, TDEE, & Calorie Deficits

Weight management comes down to energy balance. You do not need confusing fads: your body obeys the laws of thermodynamics. Here is how your daily numbers work:

🫀 BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)

The minimum baseline calories burned keeping your heart pumping, lungs breathing, cells repairing, and organs alive in bed all day. Never eat below your BMR without clinical supervision.

⚡ TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)

Your BMR plus calories burned walking, fidgeting (NEAT), exercising, and digesting meals (TEF). Eating 500 calories below TDEE burns ~3,500 kcal per week, equaling 1 pound of pure body fat loss.

The Scientific Formula (Mifflin-St Jeor)

Adopted by the American Dietetic Association as the most accurate non-clinical formula:

Men: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age) + 5
Women: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age) - 161
TDEE: BMR × Activity Multiplier (1.2 to 1.9)
