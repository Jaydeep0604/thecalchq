How is Monthly Loan EMI Calculated?

An **Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)** is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs apply to personal loans, auto loans, mortgages, and student loans.

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n - 1]
  • P (Principal): The total initial sum of money borrowed.
  • r (Periodic Monthly Interest Rate): The annual interest rate divided by 12 and then by 100.
  • n (Total Installments): The total loan duration in months (e.g., a 5-year loan = 60 months).

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I reduce my total interest paid on a loan?

Making extra principal payments each year or choosing a shorter loan term drastically reduces compounding interest.
